For the best Eema Ever!

I love you and know you do a lot for me.

— Ambrose

No. I

On the mats together

Thank you for coaching me on the mat. I love it when you help me, especially when it leads to me tapping the person I'm training with. It's even better than when Daddy cheats in bulldog and tags the other kids.

No. II

Piano recital, June 2025

It was really nice to be able to play and earn a certificate. Thank you so much for getting me music lessons, Eema. I also like that you practice with me.

No. III

Camping trip at the Jordan Mill Reservoir

Thank you so much for planning the camping trip for my birthday. I really liked going on the boat with you and paddling all the way over to Daddy. And back. Thanks for always setting fun stuff up for me.

No. IV

Beach trip

Thank you for going to Mexico with me and doing stuff with me there, Eema. I like going to the pool with you. And swimming and going bodyboarding in the ocean. I just wish there were less sea urchins. Or at least Daddy does.

No. V

World Travelers

I always like when we can travel together and go on crazy adventures. Next year I probably will be too big to ride on your back, though.

No. VI

Birthday tea time

Thanks for always getting Auntie Danak to come out and spend time with me. My favorite thing at your birthday tea time was the lemon squares.

No. VII

Hanukkah lights

My favorite thing was lighting the candles. Thanks for teaching me Hebrew. יום אמא שמח!

No. VIII

Christmas morning

Thanks for getting me the silly string. It was really fun to play with. You got me the best presents ever.

No. IX

Puzzle nights

Thank you for helping me build the hundred-piece puzzles. I couldn't have done it without you.

No. X

Lost teeth!

I'm so glad that tooth came out in the hot tub. It was hurting so badly before it came out. Thank you for organizing, selling my bones. And I'm glad that you are happy that I lost a tooth.

No. XI

That cake

Thank you for making a birthday cake for my party. The cake was so delicious. I couldn't even eat one slice.
